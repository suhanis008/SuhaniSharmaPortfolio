Origami

Flasher Hat

About This Piece

I created this flasher hat using an 8x8 in origami sheet and following Jeremy Shafer’s Baby Flasher tutorial .

My First Attempt

I had originally tried making this form from printer paper cut into a square, and this is how it turned out.

Trying Again with Better Paper

This time I decided to approach it with an 8 × 8 inch origami sheet. I first created a 16 × 16 grid, which honestly my origami folding machine could have helped with ;))

Folding Process

Then I followed the crease pattern and patiently kept folding along it while watching the video to see how things should actually be moving. Finally, after a couple of hours, I had the flasher hat in my hands.
