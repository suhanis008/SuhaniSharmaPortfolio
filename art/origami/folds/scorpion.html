Origami

Scorpion

About This Piece

I folded this scorpion using an 8 × 8 inch sheet of origami paper. This is Jo Nakashima’s model and required folding a 24 × 24 grid. This was my first time working with such a detailed grid and turning it into a full 3D structure. I’m really proud of how it turned out.

Folding Process

I started with the grid and slowly worked through the shaping until the scorpion form started coming together.
