Art

Things I Make

This is where I keep the projects I make with my hands, whether that means folding paper, sketching, building tiny objects, experimenting with light, or trying something just because it seems fun. Some of these are polished, some are messy, and some are somewhere in between, but all of them taught me something.

Paper folding

Origami

Folded models, crease patterns, paper experiments, and the occasional moment where a flat square somehow turns into something alive.

Motion

Animation

Small animation projects and visual experiments. I like how animation lets a drawing or idea move just enough to feel like it has a personality.

Making

Crafts

Random handmade things, from playful builds to projects that started with “wait, could I make that?” and then took over my desk for a while.

Light and mood

Lighting Design

Experiments with light, color, shadows, and atmosphere. I love how lighting can completely change the feeling of a space or performance.

Drawing

Sketching

Sketches, studies, doodles, and drawings I made to observe more carefully or to get an idea out of my head before it disappeared.
