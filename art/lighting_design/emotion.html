Sickly

This project was created for TDM 154: Lighting Design, where we were asked to design lighting around an assigned emotion. My assigned emotion was "sickly."

I explored this feeling through a greenish-yellow lighting palette and created a structure that felt damaged and imperfect. The broken form helped communicate discomfort and uneasiness while allowing the light itself to define the mood.
