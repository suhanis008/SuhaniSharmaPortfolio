Futile Devices

This project was created for TDM 154: Lighting Design, where we were asked to design lighting inspired by a song. I chose "Futile Devices" by Sufjan Stevens and explored the emotions and atmosphere created by the music.

I designed slow transitions using an icy blue, pink/purple, and yellow color palette to reflect the gentle and emotional quality of the song. Using a stencil, I created a flowing floral effect that changed throughout the lighting sequence.

The structure consisted of intertwined rings that were lifted slightly above the ground. This form represented repeated attempts and futile efforts, connecting the physical structure to the meaning of the song.
