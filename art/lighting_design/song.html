Futile Devices

This project was created for TDM 154: Lighting Design, where we were asked to design lighting inspired by a song. I chose "Futile Devices" by Sufjan Stevens and explored how lighting could translate the mood and emotions of the music into a physical space.

I used slow transitions with an icy blue, pink/purple, and yellow color palette to create a soft and emotional atmosphere. At one point in the sequence, I used a stencil to create a flowing floral effect that added a new visual layer to the lighting.

The structure consisted of intertwined rings that were slightly lifted from the ground. This form represented repeated attempts and futile efforts, connecting the physical design with the meaning behind the song.
