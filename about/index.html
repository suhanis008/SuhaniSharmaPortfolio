about

Hi, I’m Suhani!

I’m a first-year at Harvard College studying mechanical engineering. I like being able to design solutions to problems I see around myself.

A little more about me

I’m interested in the intersection between engineering and art. Some days that means working on a fabrication project or research question. Other days it means sketching, folding paper, playing music, or writing.

I made this website because I wanted one place to put the different things I care about. I wanted to show the process behind the final product.

I’m especially interested in mechanical design, sustainability, hands-on building, and projects that connect technical work with real-world problems.

Currently

  • Studying mechanical engineering at Harvard
  • Learning more fabrication and design tools
  • Working on research and sustainability projects
  • Trying to keep making time for music, art, and writing
